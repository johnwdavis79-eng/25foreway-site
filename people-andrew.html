Andrew O. Davis

John’s brother; co-founder of Overall Capital Partners.

Andrew O. Davis grew up in St. Louis and attended St. Louis Country Day School. He holds a B.A. from Princeton University’s Woodrow Wilson School and an M.B.A. from Harvard Business School. He began his career at Emerson Electric, the St. Louis manufacturer, and later worked in investment management at Harvard Management Company (Pioneer Institute).

In 2008 he co-founded Overall Capital Partners, a Boston private equity firm that partners with smaller North American companies (The Trustees, 2022 annual meeting program).

He has served on the board of The Trustees of Reservations, the Massachusetts land-conservation and historic-preservation trust founded in 1891 (The Trustees). He also sits on the board of the Pioneer Institute for Public Policy Research in Boston (Pioneer Institute).
