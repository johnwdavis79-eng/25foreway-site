Chris Davis

John’s brother. Main Event team member, Special Olympics medalist and pizza crustologist in training.

Chris works at Main Event, where the bowling, laser tag and arcade crowd keeps him busy. He is a Special Olympics medalist, and he is currently studying to become a pizza crustologist. Please don’t ask to see the diploma yet.

Tennis runs in the family. Chris has played on and off for years, and the USTA’s St. Louis district has written about him as a great-great-nephew of Dwight F. Davis, the founder of the Davis Cup (USTA Missouri Valley).
