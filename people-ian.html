Fly fishing · Bozeman, Montana

Ian Davis

John’s cousin; co-owner and saltwater program director of Yellow Dog Flyfishing Adventures; author and artist.

Yellow Dog

Yellow Dog Flyfishing Adventures was founded by Jim Klug and is based at 213 South Willson Avenue in Bozeman, Montana. It books fishing travel to lodges around the world. Ian is its co-owner and saltwater program director (Wild River Press).

He met Klug in Colorado in the early 1990s, when he ran Breckenridge Outfitters. Klug walked in as a sales rep and became the shop’s first customer, and the shop became Yellow Dog’s first agent fly shop (Yellow Dog, Meet the Team). By early 2005 he had sold the shop and was working full time for Yellow Dog. His first job was building its Bahamas program, “so I spent months visiting the islands” (Yellow Dog, Saltwater Reels 101). In 2006 he moved to Montana as co-owner (Wild River Press).

He trained as an artist at the School of the Art Institute of Chicago and drew the illustrations and maps for Klug’s Fly Fishing Belize (Silver Creek Press, 2014). Through the Yellow Dog Community and Conservation Foundation he has worked on school projects in the Yucatán, a community sports park on Andros Island and Hurricane Dorian relief on Grand Bahama (Wild River Press).

The books

Both of Ian’s books came out from Wild River Press in 2025, which the publisher called “the Year of the Bonefish” (Wild River Press).

A Passion for Bonefish, by Ian Davis and a Company of Anglers, is a 384-page, full-color hardcover in a 12 by 10 inch landscape format, drawing on 33 contributors. It completes the publisher’s saltwater trilogy alongside A Passion for Tarpon and A Passion for Permit (Wild River Press). Ian drew the chapter-opening illustrations himself. On the Waypoints podcast he explained that, borrowing Al Hirschfeld’s trick, he hid his own name in the water lines of each drawing (Waypoints, Episode 80). Each copy of the leather-bound collector’s edition carries an original hand-drawn remarque by Ian (Anglers Journal).

My 100 Favorite Flies for Bonefish is a 202-page, full-color softcover handbook. It pairs Ian’s close-up studio photograph of each fly with its originator’s tying recipe (Yellow Dog).
