Author · Researcher

John W. Davis

Writer of history and historical fiction, researcher, songwriter, and co-founder of ClickSmith.

The books

John W. Davis writes from documents. The House of Davis is a work of nonfiction about the St. Louis family whose name he shares: its merchants, its tennis, its public parks, its high offices and its gifts. Colonel Baldwin!, in four books, follows his maternal grandfather, Lewis W. Baldwin Jr., through 1944 and 1945 on General Patton’s supply staff. Red Ball, in three books, follows the Black drivers of one invented truck company on the Red Ball Express.

The research

Each book rests on archival work: newspapers on microfilm, museum and historical society records, Army histories, the National Archives and the Library of Congress. Every novel ends with a “What Is True” or historical note that separates the record from family memory and invention.

For Colonel Baldwin!, the anchor is Pop’s own memorial in the Princeton Alumni Weekly. It records that he worked on Patton’s staff as operations G-4 “during the push through Europe” and took part “in the rescue of the famous Lipizzaner stallions from Austria.” He stayed in the Reserve until 1962 and retired as a colonel, which is where the series takes its title.

ClickSmith

With his partner Ali, John runs ClickSmith, a full-service digital marketing agency. ClickSmith builds, markets, automates and manages digital systems for businesses: websites and custom software, paid advertising and search, and automated customer communication.
